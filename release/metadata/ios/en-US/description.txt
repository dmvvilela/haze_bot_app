A little face. A lot of feeling.

Haze turns your screen into an expressive robot companion. Tap to play, hold to cuddle, or keep a little company beside you while you focus.

EXPLORE AUREA LAB
Watch compassion, courage, and gratitude take shape in interactive experiments inspired by the Aurea System project. Replay a moment, change its interpretation, and explore how earlier experiences change the response. These are creative simulations, not biological measurements.

PLAY THE FEELINGS GAME
Haze makes a face. Can you find the matching feeling? Explore expressive faces with a playful guessing game in English and Portuguese.

MAKE ROOM TO FOCUS
Set a focus timer and let Haze keep you company. Hide the controls for a quiet face on your desk or nightstand.

MAKE HAZE YOURS
Choose eye and mouth colors, switch between light and dark themes, and enjoy one smoothly animated face with its own robot sounds and optional voice.

OPTIONAL ON-DEVICE CONVERSATION
Enable the optional AI brain and download a model to talk with Haze. Conversation processing runs on your device. The core companion, game, and Aurea Lab do not require that download.

Haze began as the face of a desk robot. Now it is a little companion for your screen.
